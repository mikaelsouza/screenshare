Privacy Policy

Applies to Screenshare (screenshare.webmikas.com) and the Webmikas Screenshare Windows desktop application published by Webmikas. Last updated: 2026.

1. No Accounts, Tracking, or Analytics

Screenshare does not require user accounts or registration, and does not use tracking cookies, advertising SDKs, behavioral profiling, or analytics telemetry.

2. Peer-to-Peer Screen & Audio Media

  • Direct peer-to-peer transmission: Shared video frames and audio streams travel directly between room participants over encrypted WebRTC (DTLS/SRTP) connections and never pass through or get recorded on Webmikas servers.
  • IP visibility between connected peers: Peer connections use Cloudflare STUN (stun:stun.cloudflare.com:3478) for NAT traversal when at least one participant is sharing. Participants with whom you establish a media connection can see your IP address.
  • Desktop capture & window audio: On Windows, the Webmikas Screenshare desktop app enumerates open local windows to render the capture picker and follow supported multi-window applications, and captures system or per-process audio locally via Windows WASAPI loopback. Window titles and audio/video streams remain local except for the stream you actively choose to share with peers in your room.

3. Temporary Signalling & Room Moderation Data

  • What the signalling server sees: While you are connected to a room, our Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects signalling service processes your IP address (used for connection rate limiting and host moderation enforcement), the 8-character room code, your chosen display name, optional room password verification, and random session tokens (hostToken and rejoinToken). It never inspects SDP media contents or sees video/audio frames.
  • Automatic erasure: All room state, membership lists, and session tokens are erased automatically 12 hours after a room becomes empty.
  • Host kick bans: If a room host removes a participant, that participant's IPv4 address or IPv6 /64 prefix is temporarily blocked from joining that room for 15 minutes and cleared during ban cleanup or room expiration.

4. Local Storage & App Updates

Your browser or desktop app stores only lightweight local preferences (such as your chosen display name, temporary room session tokens for reconnection, and standard window state) on your device.

Versions installed through the Microsoft Store are updated automatically by the Microsoft Store and disable standalone external self-updating. Standalone installers downloaded from GitHub Releases periodically check GitHub Releases for newer versions.

5. Data Sharing & Contact

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties for marketing or advertising. For privacy questions or to report an issue, please open a report at github.com/mikaelsouza/screenshare/issues.
