Privacy Policy

Applies to Screenshare (screenshare.webmikas.com) and the Webmikas Screenshare Windows desktop application published by Webmikas. Last updated: 2026.

1. No Accounts, Tracking, or Analytics

Screenshare does not require user accounts or registration, and does not use tracking cookies, advertising SDKs, behavioral profiling, or analytics telemetry.

2. Peer-to-Peer Screen & Audio Media

  • Direct peer-to-peer transmission: Shared video frames and audio streams travel directly between room participants over encrypted WebRTC (DTLS/SRTP) connections and never pass through or get recorded on Webmikas servers.
  • IP visibility between connected peers: Peer connections use Cloudflare STUN (stun:stun.cloudflare.com:3478) for NAT traversal when at least one participant is sharing. Participants with whom you establish a media connection can see your IP address.
  • Desktop capture & window audio: On Windows, the Webmikas Screenshare desktop app enumerates open local windows to render the capture picker and follow supported multi-window applications, and captures system or per-process audio locally via Windows WASAPI loopback. Window titles and audio/video streams remain local except for the stream you actively choose to share with peers in your room.

3. Temporary Signalling & Room Moderation Data

  • What the signalling server sees: While you are connected to a room, our Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects signalling service processes your IPv4 address or IPv6 /64 prefix (used for connection rate limiting, brute-force protection, and host moderation enforcement), the 8-character room code, your chosen display name, session tokens (hostToken and rejoinToken) presented on join along with salted SHA-256 hashes of room secrets and tokens, a temporary promoted hostToken recovery record until claimed on reconnect, and the active room invite secret (retained only while the room exists so unlocked rooms and promoted hosts can share invites). It never inspects SDP media contents or sees video/audio frames.
  • Room chat: Plain-text room chat messages are relayed ephemerally in transit over the encrypted WebSocket (wss://) only to currently connected participants, with zero server storage or message history retention.
  • Invite URL scrubbing & token lifetimes: Invite links carry #CODE-SECRET in the URL fragment only (never transmitted in HTTP requests), and the app immediately scrubs the browser address bar to / while keeping temporary rejoinToken state in sessionStorage (for page refreshes). Room creators also store a bounded hostToken in localStorage (host-rooms, up to 20 rooms, 90-day local expiry) that grants creator host re-entry and recovers the current invite link—cleared per room inside the Invite menu via Forget host on this device or across all rooms in the lobby via Clear saved rooms; temporary session-promoted hosts keep their hostToken in memory only. For passwordless rejoin, the app also saves up to 20 recent rooms in localStorage (recent-rooms, 90-day local expiry) containing the room code, role, last-visited timestamp, latest invite secret (when unlocked or hosted), optional local nickname, and up to 4 last-seen participant display names. You can remove individual entries, wipe both recent-rooms and host-rooms with Clear saved rooms, or disable saving on shared devices via Don't save rooms on this device (recent-rooms-disabled). Disconnected rejoinToken tickets expire 5 minutes after leaving (or 25 seconds after a manual invite rotation); if no host is present in an occupied room for 5 minutes, the earliest-joined active participant is temporarily promoted for that session without unlocking occupied locked rooms.
  • Automatic erasure: Unused rooms that are never joined expire automatically after 12 hours. Once a used room empties, ephemeral session tokens, stored IPv4 / IPv6 /64 prefixes (membership ticket and kick-ban maps, each capped at 128 entries), pending or session host promotions, and room locks are erased automatically 15 minutes after the room becomes empty (rotating the invite secret if the room was locked when emptied so pre-lock leaked links stay invalid, and restoring the creator’s host authority). Room invite credentials (roomCode, roomPassword / passwordHash, and creator hostTokenHash) are retained for up to 90 days from the creator’s last activity (non-host visits do not extend this cap, and rotated or kicked secrets stay revoked), then erased automatically. Uninitialized room probes perform zero persistent storage writes.
  • Host kick bans & auto-rotation: If a room host removes a participant, all disconnected rejoin tickets across the room are revoked (remaining connected participants receive fresh tickets), the removed participant's IPv4 address or IPv6 /64 prefix is temporarily blocked from that room for 15 minutes, and the room's invite secret is automatically rotated (omitted for non-hosts whenever the room is locked).

4. Local Storage & App Updates

Your browser or desktop app stores only lightweight local preferences and rejoin data on your device: your chosen display name, temporary sessionStorage rejoin tokens, bounded localStorage creator hostToken credentials (host-rooms, up to 20 rooms, 90-day expiry) that can recover a hosted room’s invite link, bounded localStorage recent rooms (recent-rooms, up to 20 rooms, 90-day expiry, storing room codes, roles, invite secrets, optional local nicknames, and up to 4 last-seen participant display names for passwordless rejoin), your shared-device opt-out setting (recent-rooms-disabled), and standard window state. You can opt out anytime via Don't save rooms on this device, wipe all saved recent rooms and host tokens via Clear saved rooms, or remove a single room’s host access in-room via Forget host on this device.

Standalone desktop installers downloaded from GitHub Releases periodically check GitHub Releases for newer versions.

5. Data Sharing & Contact

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties for marketing or advertising. For privacy questions or to report an issue, please open a report at github.com/mikaelsouza/screenshare/issues.
